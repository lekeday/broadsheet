Calculation & Position Sorting Notes

• TOTAL: Computed as sum of all 11 subject continuous assessment + exam scores (Max: 1100 marks).
• B/F: Brought forward marks from prior terms.
• GRAND TOTAL: Current Term Total + B/F.
• AVERAGE%: Current Term Total ÷ 11 subjects (rounded to 1 decimal place).
• SORT TO FIND POSITION: Click "Sort by Position" or any column header to rearrange pupils with standard competition ranking (ties share rank).
